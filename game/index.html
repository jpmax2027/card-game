How to play

  • Goal: reduce King Roderick to 0 life before he does the same to you.
  • Energy: you get 3 energy each turn. Every card shows its cost in the blue gem.
  • Attack ♠ deals damage. Block ♣ absorbs damage until your next turn.
  • Heal ♥ restores life, up to 30. Special ♦ cards draw, drain or focus.
  • At the end of your turn the King plays his hand, then you draw up to 5 cards.
  • When your deck runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back in.
  • Keys: 1–7 play a card · Space ends turn · R restarts · H shows this help.
